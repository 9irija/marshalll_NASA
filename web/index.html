Welcome to Jezero crater

Plan a walk on Mars in four steps

  1. Pick a destination on the left, or click a white pin on the map. Choose walking or the rover.
  2. Compare the three routes. Each is priced in oxygen and battery, not just distance.
  3. Press play. The teal area is everywhere you can still get home from. Watch it shrink as the EVA clock runs.
  4. Test the plan. Inject a dust storm or solar alert, try the Helmet HUD, or let the ML find more ground like your site.

Every number comes from real NASA data and a tested planner. Space bar plays and pauses; Esc closes panels.
